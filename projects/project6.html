Project Overview

Pharmaceutical organizations generate large volumes of commercial data across sales representatives, doctors, products, and marketing campaigns. This project simulates a real-world commercial analytics platform that enables leadership to monitor KPIs, evaluate campaign performance, identify high-performing products, and support data-driven decision-making.

Domain: Pharmaceutical Sales Analytics | Dataset Size (10,000 Sales Records, 100 Products, 50 Representatives, 200 Doctors)

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: Sales logs, doctor engagement records, and marketing campaign costs were completely disconnected, blocking comprehensive reporting

Solution:Designed a centralized, star-schema relational model in MySQL to cleanly link disparate data streams into a single source of truth.

Challenge: It was highly difficult to prove whether localized prescription spikes were caused by specific marketing investments or field rep visits

Solution: Developed custom DAX metrics in Power BI to tie campaign spend timelines directly to localized prescription trends, isolating exact campaign ROI.

Challenge: Sales representative logs were heavily unstructured, making it nearly impossible to evaluate overall field effectiveness and target coverage

Solution: Used Python (Pandas) to parse and normalize field logs, converting raw text and activity counts into quantifiable performance metrics.

Results & Impact

The platform successfully launched and has been serving internal commercial teams. Key achievements include:

  • Enhanced Sales Efficiency: Shifted team focus from raw call volumes to optimized, structured territory planning and better field performance self-analysis.
  • Actionable Commercial Insights: Transformed raw sales, prescription, and distraction logs into clear, personalized behavioral metrics for sales representative improvement
  • Automated Feedback Loop: Delivered real-time, tailored marketing suggestions and ideal physician engagement timing recommendations through integrated data models.
  • Secure Data Management: Guaranteed business privacy and safe record handling using encrypted, token-based authentication and secure relational database storage.
  • Scalable Architecture Deployment: Established a responsive, full-stack data intelligence framework capable of expanding to include more complex tracking metrics and additional business KPIs.

Key Learnings
